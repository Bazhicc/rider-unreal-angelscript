# Third-party sources

The AngelScript language server, grammar and VS Code extension originate from
[Hazelight/vscode-unreal-angelscript](https://github.com/Hazelight/vscode-unreal-angelscript),
revision `ffc7ab2` (1.9.4). Their MIT license and copyright notice remain in `LICENSE`.

The Rider integration in `src/main`, and the Gradle wrapper, were adapted from
[aesirinteractive/rider-unreal-angelscript](https://github.com/aesirinteractive/rider-unreal-angelscript),
revision `bf2841c37a3e60326b3f1dde2a4418a3bff33e2f`. The original package names and
source notices are retained to identify that provenance. That revision contains
no top-level license file; the Hazelight MIT notice does not establish the
license of the separately authored Rider integration.

Gradle is licensed under Apache License 2.0:
https://github.com/gradle/gradle/blob/v9.0.0/LICENSE
